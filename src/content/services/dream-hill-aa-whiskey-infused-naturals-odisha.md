## About Dream Hill AA Whiskey Infused Naturals

Dream Hill sits in the Koraput hills of the Eastern Ghats, Odisha. For this lot the whole cherry is fermented in contact with whiskey-soaked casks before it goes onto raised beds to sun-dry. The barrel character carries through the dry to give an aromatic, boozy sweetness layered over the fruit of a natural process.

## Flavour profile

- **Aroma:** Oak, whiskey, honey
- **Taste:** A hint of whiskey, honey, mandarin
- **Finish:** Long, warm, aromatic
- **Body:** Medium-full — best as pour-over, Aeropress or French press
- **Acidity:** Medium, carrying intense flavour

## Best brewing methods

| Method | Grind | Ratio |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Pour-over | Medium-fine | 1 : 16 |
| Aeropress | Medium | 1 : 15 |
| French press | Coarse | 1 : 13 |

## Sourcing & sustainability

Sourced directly from the Dream Hill estate. Infused lots are produced in small seasonal batches, so availability varies with the harvest.

## Storage

Store in an airtight container away from light and heat. Whole beans stay fresh for up to 30 days after roasting; green beans, stored cool and dry, hold for 6–12 months.
