## About the Attikan Arabica Chikmagalur Robusta Blend

Washed Commercial-grade Arabica from Attikan Estate in the Mudigere hills of Chikmagalur, fully de-pulped and dried without residual mucilage for a clean, low-fuss cup. A 5% measure of naturally processed Chikmagalur Robusta adds body and a fuller finish — a reliable, everyday blend.

## Flavour profile

- **Aroma:** Light roasted nuts, faint citrus
- **Taste:** Clean acidity, mild sweetness, toasted almond
- **Finish:** Short and clean
- **Body:** Medium — works equally well as filter coffee or espresso

## Best brewing methods

| Method | Grind | Ratio |
|--------|-------|-------|
| South Indian filter | Very fine | 1 : 5 |
| Pour-over | Medium | 1 : 15 |
| Moka pot | Fine-medium | 1 : 7 |

## Sourcing & sustainability

Sourced directly from Attikan Estate and the surrounding Mudigere–Koppa belt of Chikmagalur, one of four estate lots we work with in the region alongside Biccode, Gungeri, and Karadykan.

## Storage

Store in an airtight container away from light and heat. Best enjoyed within 30 days of the roast date printed on the bag; grind just before brewing.
