## About the Halflong Arabica Tirap Robusta Blend

This blend brings together two naturally processed lots from the North East — a hilly, high-rainfall belt that's still a small and emerging part of India's coffee map. The base is a Specialty-grade Arabica grown around Halflong in the Dima Hasao hills of Assam, cherry-dried in the sun to build up fruit sweetness. A 5% measure of naturally processed Commercial Robusta from Tirap, Arunachal Pradesh, is folded in to add body and a longer, rounder finish.

Because both components are natural-processed, the cup leans sweet and fruit-forward rather than clean and bright — closer to a dessert-style cup than a filter-bright one.

## Flavour profile

- **Aroma:** Ripe red fruit, cocoa, molasses
- **Taste:** Dark chocolate, brown sugar, a hint of stewed fruit
- **Finish:** Full, sweet, and lingering
- **Body:** Full — well suited to milk-based brews as well as black filter coffee

## Sourcing & sustainability

The Arabica is sourced from small-holder farms around Halflong; the Robusta from a Nocte farmer collective in Tirap's Khonsa block. Both regions are early in their coffee-growing history, and direct sourcing at a fair premium helps fund shared processing infrastructure for these communities.

## Storage

Store in an airtight container away from light and heat. Best enjoyed within 30 days of the roast date printed on the bag; grind just before brewing.
