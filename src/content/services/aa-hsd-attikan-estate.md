## About Attikan Estate AA Honey Sun-dried

Attikan Estate sits in the Mudigere hills of Chikkamagalur, Karnataka. This lot is screened to AA — the largest, most even bean size — and carries a Specialty cup score.

The cherry is honey sun-dried: pulped, then dried on raised beds with a portion of its sticky mucilage left on the parchment. That extra sugar contact during drying gives the cup a bright, tropical juiciness without tipping into the heavier fruit of a full natural.

## Flavour profile

- **Aroma:** Pink grapefruit, ripe grape
- **Taste:** Tropical fruit, grapefruit, kiwi, clean sweetness
- **Finish:** Bright, medium-length, gently sweet
- **Body:** Medium — excellent as pour-over or Aeropress
- **Acidity:** Bright

## Best brewing methods

| Method | Grind | Ratio |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Pour-over | Medium-fine | 1 : 16 |
| Aeropress | Medium | 1 : 15 |
| South Indian filter | Very fine | 1 : 5 |

## Sourcing & sustainability

Sourced directly from Attikan Estate and the surrounding Mudigere–Koppa belt of Chikkamagalur, one of the estate lots we work with in the region alongside Karadykan and Gungeri.

## Storage

Store in an airtight container away from light and heat. Whole beans stay fresh for up to 30 days after roasting; green beans, stored cool and dry, hold for 6–12 months.
