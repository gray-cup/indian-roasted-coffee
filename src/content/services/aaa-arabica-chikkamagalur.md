## About AAA Arabica Chikkamagalur

Grown at Attikan Estate in the Mudigere hills of Chikkamagalur, Karnataka, this lot is screened to AAA — the largest and most even bean size, above the AA grade. Larger beans roast more evenly and carry a cleaner, sweeter cup, which is why this grade sits at the top of our Chikkamagalur Arabica line-up — priced higher than our Chikmagalur Robusta.

The cherry is washed: pulped and fermented before drying, giving a clean, bright base cup with none of the fruit-forward funk of a natural process.

## Flavour profile

- **Aroma:** Floral, honeyed
- **Taste:** Citrus, honey sweetness, delicate florals
- **Finish:** Clean, bright, medium-length
- **Body:** Medium-light — best as pour-over or Aeropress
- **Acidity:** Bright

## Best brewing methods

| Method | Grind | Ratio |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Pour-over | Medium-fine | 1 : 16 |
| Aeropress | Medium | 1 : 15 |
| South Indian filter | Very fine | 1 : 5 |

## Sourcing & sustainability

Sourced directly from Attikan Estate in the Mudigere–Koppa belt of Chikkamagalur, the same estate lot behind our Attikan AA Honey Sun-dried, screened here to the larger AAA grade.

## Storage

Store in an airtight container away from light and heat. Whole beans stay fresh for up to 30 days after roasting; green beans, stored cool and dry, hold for 6–12 months.
