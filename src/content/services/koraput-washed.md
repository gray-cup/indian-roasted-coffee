## About Koraput Washed

Koraput sits in the Eastern Ghats of southern Odisha — a district of dense forest, deep valleys, and plateau land that rarely exceeds 1,200 metres. For generations, Kondh, Paraja, and Gadaba tribal communities have cultivated Arabica under the shade of native sal and jackfruit trees, with little intervention from fertilisers or pesticides.

This lot is fully washed — the cherry skin and mucilage are removed before drying — which strips away fruit-layer influence and lets the bean's inherent clarity shine through. The result is a clean, bright cup with well-defined acidity, guava sweetness, and a light floral finish.

## Flavour profile

- **Aroma:** Guava, citrus blossom, light florals
- **Taste:** Bright citrus, guava, a clean sweetness
- **Finish:** Crisp, clean, and well-defined
- **Body:** Light-medium — works beautifully as pour-over or Aeropress

## Sourcing & sustainability

Beans are sourced directly from tribal farmer groups in the Koraput and Narayanpatna blocks under a fair-price procurement model facilitated by Odisha government-backed farmer producer organisations. Shade-grown cultivation preserves the Eastern Ghats' biodiversity corridor and provides supplementary income to communities whose primary livelihood is subsistence agriculture.

## Storage

Store in an airtight container away from light and heat. Whole beans stay fresh for up to 30 days; once ground, use within 7 days for the best flavour.
