## About the Dream Hill Koraput Arabica Chikmagalur Robusta Honey Blend

Dream Hill is a small estate in the Koraput hills of Odisha, growing Specialty-grade Arabica that's honey-processed — the cherry skin is removed but a sticky layer of mucilage is left on the parchment as it dries in the sun. That extra sugar contact gives the coffee a syrupy sweetness and rounder body than a fully washed lot. A 5% measure of naturally processed Robusta from Chikmagalur, Karnataka, adds extra weight and a longer finish.

## Flavour profile

- **Aroma:** Honey, ripe stone fruit, warm caramel
- **Taste:** Caramel sweetness, peach, a soft cocoa undertone
- **Finish:** Long, syrupy, and smooth
- **Body:** Medium-full — excellent as a straight-up filter or Aeropress cup

## Best brewing methods

| Method | Grind | Ratio |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Pour-over | Medium | 1 : 15 |
| Aeropress | Medium-fine | 1 : 14 |
| South Indian filter | Very fine | 1 : 5 |

## Sourcing & sustainability

Beans are sourced directly from the Dream Hill estate in Koraput, with the Robusta component from the Mudigere and Koppa regions of Chikmagalur. Honey-processing lots are produced in limited seasonal batches, so availability can vary with the harvest.

## Storage

Store in an airtight container away from light and heat. Best enjoyed within 30 days of the roast date printed on the bag; grind just before brewing.
