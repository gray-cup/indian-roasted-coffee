## About the Koraput Arabica Chikmagalur Robusta Blend

An everyday, easy-drinking blend built on washed Arabica from the tribal highlands of Koraput, Odisha. Washing strips away the fruit pulp before drying, so the base cup is clean and mild rather than fruity. A 5% measure of naturally processed Robusta from Chikkamagalur, Karnataka, is added for body and a touch of crema-friendly weight — useful whether you're brewing filter coffee or espresso.

This is priced and positioned as a Commercial-grade daily driver: consistent, approachable, and a good starting point if you're new to single-origin Indian coffee.

## Flavour profile

- **Aroma:** Light citrus, roasted nuts
- **Taste:** Mild sweetness, toasted almond, soft acidity
- **Finish:** Clean and short
- **Body:** Medium — versatile across filter and milk-based brews

## Best brewing methods

| Method | Grind | Ratio |
|--------|-------|-------|
| South Indian filter | Very fine | 1 : 5 |
| Pour-over | Medium | 1 : 15 |
| French press | Coarse | 1 : 13 |

## Sourcing & sustainability

The Arabica comes from tribal farmer cooperatives in the Koraput district hills; the Robusta from the Mudigere and Koppa regions of Chikkamagalur. Both are long-standing, well-established growing regions with reliable seasonal supply.

## Storage

Store in an airtight container away from light and heat. Best enjoyed within 30 days of the roast date printed on the bag; grind just before brewing.
