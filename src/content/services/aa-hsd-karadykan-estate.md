## About Karadykan Estate AA Honey Sun-dried

Karadykan Estate lies in the Mudigere–Koppa belt of Chikkamagalur, Karnataka. This lot is screened to AA — the largest, most even bean size — and carries a Specialty cup score.

The cherry is honey sun-dried: pulped, then dried on raised beds with a portion of its sticky mucilage left on the parchment. The extra sugar contact during drying builds a bright citrus-and-banana sweetness while keeping the cup clean.

## Flavour profile

- **Aroma:** Lemon zest, ripe banana
- **Taste:** Citrus, banana, tropical fruit, kiwi
- **Finish:** Clean, medium-length, gently sweet
- **Body:** Medium — excellent as pour-over or Aeropress
- **Acidity:** Medium

## Best brewing methods

| Method | Grind | Ratio |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Pour-over | Medium-fine | 1 : 16 |
| Aeropress | Medium | 1 : 15 |
| South Indian filter | Very fine | 1 : 5 |

## Sourcing & sustainability

Sourced directly from Karadykan Estate, one of the estate lots we work with in the Mudigere–Koppa belt of Chikkamagalur alongside Attikan and Gungeri.

## Storage

Store in an airtight container away from light and heat. Whole beans stay fresh for up to 30 days after roasting; green beans, stored cool and dry, hold for 6–12 months.
