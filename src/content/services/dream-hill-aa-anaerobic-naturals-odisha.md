## About Dream Hill AA Anaerobic Naturals

Dream Hill sits in the Koraput hills of the Eastern Ghats, Odisha. For this lot the whole cherry is sealed in tanks to ferment without oxygen before it goes onto raised beds to sun-dry. The anaerobic stage pushes the fruit character well past a standard natural — bright, layered, and intense in the cup.

## Flavour profile

- **Aroma:** Stewed plum, tropical fruit
- **Taste:** Sour plum, guava, a hint of honey, flecks of papaya
- **Finish:** Long, juicy, gently tart
- **Body:** Medium-full — best as pour-over or Aeropress
- **Acidity:** Medium, carrying intense fruit

## Best brewing methods

| Method | Grind | Ratio |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Pour-over | Medium-fine | 1 : 16 |
| Aeropress | Medium | 1 : 15 |
| French press | Coarse | 1 : 13 |

## Sourcing & sustainability

Sourced directly from the Dream Hill estate. Anaerobic lots are produced in small seasonal batches, so availability varies with the harvest.

## Storage

Store in an airtight container away from light and heat. Whole beans stay fresh for up to 30 days after roasting; green beans, stored cool and dry, hold for 6–12 months.
