## About the Classic 70/30 Blend

Our Classic Blend combines 70% washed Arabica from the high-altitude estates of Coorg with 30% natural-processed Robusta from Chikmagalur. The Arabica brings brightness and floral sweetness, while the Robusta adds body, crema, and a deep chocolate backbone — the everyday blend for espresso, filter, and milk-based drinks alike.

Beans are roasted whole and shipped whole by default; select a grind at checkout (whole bean, coarse, medium, or fine) if you'd rather we grind it for you.

## Flavour profile

- **Aroma:** Roasted cocoa, toasted nuts, light caramel
- **Taste:** Dark chocolate, hazelnut, a mellow sweetness
- **Finish:** Full, lingering, low acidity
- **Body:** Full — excellent for espresso, filter coffee, and cold brew

## Best brewing methods

| Method | Grind | Ratio |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Espresso | Fine | 1 : 2 |
| South Indian filter | Fine-medium | 1 : 8 |
| Pour-over | Medium | 1 : 16 |
| Cold brew | Coarse | 1 : 8 |

## Sourcing & sustainability

Arabica is sourced from small family estates around Madikeri, and Robusta from shade-grown plots near Chikmagalur — both paid above Fair Trade floor prices. Blending happens only after roasting, so each component is roasted to the profile that suits it best before being combined.

## Storage

Store in an airtight container away from light and heat. Whole beans stay fresh for up to 30 days; once ground, use within 7 days for the best flavour.
