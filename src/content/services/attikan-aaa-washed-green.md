## About Attikan AAA Washed Green Coffee

Washed AAA-screened Arabica from Attikan Estate in the Mudigere hills of Chikmagalur — the same lot behind our Attikan blends, offered here as unroasted green beans for home and commercial roasters. Fully de-pulped and sun-dried without residual mucilage for a clean, predictable roast.

## Storage

Store green beans in a breathable bag in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Properly stored green coffee holds its quality for 6–12 months.
