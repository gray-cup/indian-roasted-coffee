## About the Attikan Arabica Chikmagalur Robusta Honey Blend

Attikan Estate sits in the Mudigere hills of Chikmagalur, one of four estates we work with in the region. This lot is honey sun-dried — parchment coffee dried with a portion of its natural mucilage still intact — giving it a rounder, sweeter cup than a washed lot from the same estate. A 5% measure of naturally processed Chikmagalur Robusta rounds out the body.

## Flavour profile

- **Aroma:** Honey, red berry, warm cocoa
- **Taste:** Milk chocolate, red fruit sweetness, gentle acidity
- **Finish:** Smooth and rounded
- **Body:** Full — a good all-day cup, from filter to French press

## Best brewing methods

| Method | Grind | Ratio |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Pour-over | Medium | 1 : 15 |
| French press | Coarse | 1 : 13 |
| South Indian filter | Very fine | 1 : 5 |

## Sourcing & sustainability

Sourced directly from Attikan Estate and the surrounding Mudigere–Koppa belt of Chikmagalur. Estates in this cluster practise shade-growing under native tree cover, which supports soil health and biodiversity between harvests.

## Storage

Store in an airtight container away from light and heat. Best enjoyed within 30 days of the roast date printed on the bag; grind just before brewing.
