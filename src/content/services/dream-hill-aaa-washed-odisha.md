## About Dream Hill AAA Washed Arabica

Dream Hill is a small estate in the Koraput hills of the Eastern Ghats, Odisha, where tribal farming communities grow Arabica under native shade at altitude. This lot is fully washed — the cherry skin and mucilage removed before drying — and screened to a large, even AAA bean size for a clean, predictable roast.

## Flavour profile

- **Aroma:** Cocoa, light honey
- **Taste:** Chocolate, toasted nuts, a soft honeyed sweetness
- **Finish:** Clean, medium-length, nutty
- **Body:** Medium — works as filter coffee or espresso
- **Acidity:** Medium

## Best brewing methods

| Method | Grind | Ratio |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Pour-over | Medium | 1 : 16 |
| South Indian filter | Very fine | 1 : 5 |
| Aeropress | Medium | 1 : 15 |

## Sourcing & sustainability

Sourced directly from the Dream Hill estate through a fair-price procurement model. Shade-grown cultivation preserves the Eastern Ghats' biodiversity corridor and provides supplementary income to communities whose primary livelihood is subsistence agriculture.

## Storage

Store in an airtight container away from light and heat. Whole beans stay fresh for up to 30 days after roasting; green beans, stored cool and dry, hold for 6–12 months.
