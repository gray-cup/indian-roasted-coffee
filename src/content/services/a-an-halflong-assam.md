## About Halflong A Arabica Naturals

Haflong — the only hill station in Assam — rises above the Barak Valley in the Dima Hasao district. Small Dimasa and Zeme Naga communities have grown Arabica on the steeper slopes here for decades, away from the lowland humidity that defines most of Assam.

This is an A-grade single-origin lot, naturally processed: the whole cherry dries on raised beds over three to four weeks, so the fruit sugars concentrate into the bean. The result is a berry-forward sweetness with the light sour tang that a good natural keeps.

## Flavour profile

- **Aroma:** Ripe strawberry, red currant
- **Taste:** Sweetness of ripe berries, a slight tang of sourness
- **Finish:** Medium, fruity, gently tart
- **Body:** Medium — good as pour-over, Aeropress or French press

## Best brewing methods

| Method | Grind | Ratio |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Pour-over | Medium-fine | 1 : 16 |
| Aeropress | Medium | 1 : 15 |
| French press | Coarse | 1 : 13 |

## Sourcing & sustainability

Procurement is through the Dima Hasao district farmer collective, which trains small-holders in post-harvest processing and provides price stability through multi-season contracts. Coffee cultivation acts as an economic buffer for communities that otherwise depend on a single tea season.

## Storage

Store in an airtight container away from light and heat. Whole beans remain at peak quality for up to 45 days after the roast date.
