## About Brown Valley AA Honey Sun-dried

Brown Valley lies in the Eastern Ghats of Odisha, where tribal farming communities grow Arabica under native shade at altitude. This lot is screened to AA — the largest, most even bean size — and carries a Specialty cup score.

The cherry is honey sun-dried: pulped, then dried on raised beds with a portion of its sticky mucilage still on the parchment. That extra sugar contact during drying is what gives the cup its citrus-forward juiciness without tipping into the heavier fruit of a full natural.

## Flavour profile

- **Aroma:** Mandarin peel, orange blossom
- **Taste:** Sweet lime, green grape, clean citrus sweetness
- **Finish:** Bright, medium-length, gently sweet
- **Body:** Medium — excellent as pour-over or Aeropress
- **Acidity:** Medium

## Best brewing methods

| Method | Grind | Ratio |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Pour-over | Medium-fine | 1 : 16 |
| Aeropress | Medium | 1 : 15 |
| South Indian filter | Very fine | 1 : 5 |

## Sourcing & sustainability

Sourced directly from farmer groups in the Brown Valley belt of Odisha through a fair-price procurement model. Shade-grown cultivation preserves the Eastern Ghats' biodiversity corridor and provides supplementary income to communities whose primary livelihood is subsistence agriculture.

## Storage

Store in an airtight container away from light and heat. Whole beans stay fresh for up to 30 days; once ground, use within 7 days for the best flavour.
